Services

Build, launch, clarify, or reset your go-to-market system.

Each engagement is built for B2B companies selling complex technology into complex buying committees.

First Marketing Engine

For technical founders who have product traction but no scalable marketing function. I build the positioning, ICP, campaign plan, reporting model, content foundation, and operating cadence needed to move beyond founder-led sales.

ABM Strategy & Architecture

For B2B companies that need to establish or reset account-based marketing. I help define target account tiers, buying committees, account journeys, orchestration plays, content needs, sales alignment, and measurement.

Product Narrative & Category Education

For complex products that require market education. I help clarify the story, translate technical value into buyer relevance, and build content that helps prospects understand why change matters.

GTM Audit & Marketing Ops Reset

For established teams with underperforming campaigns, unclear attribution, scattered tools, or weak sales and marketing alignment. I diagnose the system, identify gaps, and build a practical roadmap.

About

I help technical companies turn complex products into market clarity, account strategy, and revenue-ready marketing systems.

I am a B2B marketing strategist and GTM operator who helps technical companies build the marketing foundations needed for growth. My work sits at the intersection of positioning, ABM, content strategy, demand generation, sales alignment, and marketing operations.

I have led marketing growth programs at Swift Medical, launched account-based marketing programs at Mercatus and Nulogy, and designed growth and brand strategy for RideCo.

I work best with founder-led and growth-stage companies where the product is strong, the market is complex, and the marketing function needs senior-level structure without unnecessary overhead.
